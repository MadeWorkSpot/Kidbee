FAMILY GUIDELINES

Rules and Regulations

These sample guidelines help us provide a safe, caring, and happy environment for every child.

KidBee Day Care Guidelines

  1. Attendance and timing: Please bring and collect your child within the agreed care hours. Inform the centre as soon as possible about absences or delays.
  2. Safe pickup: Children may be collected only by a parent, guardian, or person listed in the pickup authorization section. Photo identification may be requested.
  3. Health and wellness: Do not send a child who has a fever, contagious illness, or symptoms requiring close medical care. Please share relevant health and allergy information with staff.
  4. Medication: Medication will be administered only when it is provided with written instructions and the required parent or guardian authorization.
  5. Respectful conduct: Children, families, and staff are expected to treat one another with kindness and respect. Bullying, unsafe behaviour, and abusive language are not permitted.
  6. Personal belongings: Label all belongings brought to the centre. KidBee is not responsible for items that are lost or damaged.
  7. Communication: Parents and guardians must keep their contact details and emergency contact information up to date and respond promptly to urgent calls.
  8. Fees and closure: Care fees, late pickup charges, holidays, and closure notices will be communicated by the centre and are subject to the applicable enrolment terms.
  9. Safety first: Please follow all instructions from KidBee staff during arrivals, departures, emergency drills, and other safety procedures.

Important: These are sample rules and regulations provided for the admission form. KidBee Day Care may update them when needed and will communicate material changes to families.
