# KidBee Day Care 🐝

A website for **KidBee Day Care & Early Learning Center**, designed to provide parents and guardians with information about childcare programs and early childhood education.

## 📌 What It Is
KidBee is an informative and interactive web portal for a nursery and daycare facility offering infant care, toddler learning, and preschool activities.

## 🎯 What It Is Used For
- **Showcasing Programs & Facilities:** Introduces parents to the curriculum, safety standards, daily schedules, and learning environment.
- **Online Admission Registration:** Allows parents to submit student enrollment inquiries and admission forms online.
- **Photo & Activity Gallery:** Displays daily activities, classrooms, and event highlights to prospective families.
- **Inquiries & Contact:** Provides essential contact details, operating hours, and location information for easy parent-teacher communication.
